My fellow citizens,

We stand at the beginning of a transformative era. New technologies,
ranging from artificial intelligence to advanced renewable systems, are
reshaping the foundations of global power. This is a moment of possibility
unlike anything our country has seen in generations.

But with these opportunities come unprecedented risks. Hostile actors are
testing our cyber defenses every day. Entire industries are vulnerable to
disruption by foreign competitors who do not share our standards or our
values. Misinformation is spreading at a pace our institutions were never
designed to handle. If we do not act decisively, we risk losing both our
economic leadership and our democratic stability.

Therefore, I am announcing a comprehensive national resilience package.
It will establish mandatory certification for all high-risk technologies,
expand the authority of security agencies to intervene in critical sectors,
and require all major platforms to comply with new transparency and auditing
standards. These measures are not intended to limit innovation, but to
ensure that innovation strengthens, rather than weakens, our society.

To our entrepreneurs and scientists: you are the engine of our future. We
believe in your creativity and your ambition. But ambition without guardrails
can put us all in danger. Safeguards are not an obstacle to progress; they
are the framework that allows progress to endure.

Some critics will claim that these reforms go too far. They will argue that
regulation stifles growth. But history shows that nations thrive when they
combine bold vision with strong foundations. The world is moving fast, and
we cannot afford hesitation. We must both innovate and protect, advance and
defend.

This is not a choice between freedom and security. It is a commitment to
ensuring that our freedoms survive the challenges ahead. Together, we will
build a country that leads in innovation while remaining strong, stable,
and united.

Thank you.
