Our nation stands at the threshold of unprecedented technological growth. 
Artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and autonomous systems promise 
extraordinary breakthroughs that can elevate our economy and transform 
public services for the better.

But these opportunities come with escalating risks that cannot be ignored. 
Unchecked AI deployment has already led to multiple security breaches, 
large-scale misinformation incidents, and visible destabilization in 
critical sectors. The speed of innovation is now outpacing our existing 
institutions’ ability to manage it responsibly.

For this reason, we must introduce a comprehensive legal framework that 
mandates pre-deployment risk assessments, emergency-stop mechanisms for 
high-risk models, and strict transparency obligations for all companies 
developing advanced AI systems. These measures are not designed to slow 
progress, but to preserve the stable foundation on which sustainable 
innovation can occur.

We fully support scientific creativity, competitive markets, and 
entrepreneurial ambition. However, to ensure that these innovations 
benefit society rather than undermine it, we must first establish clear 
boundaries and enforceable standards. Stability is not the enemy of 
progress; it is the condition that makes real progress possible.
